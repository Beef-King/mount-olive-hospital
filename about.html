Our Story

A private hospital built around the neighbourhood it serves

Mount Olive Hospital & Maternity has grown alongside the families of Uyo, offering general outpatient care, maternity services and everyday medical attention close to home. We believe good healthcare starts with being known — by name, not by file number.

Our team of doctors, nurses and midwives works to keep waiting times short and communication honest, so every visit feels less like a system and more like a relationship.
