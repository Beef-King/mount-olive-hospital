Our Services

General Outpatient

Walk-in and scheduled consultations for common illness, check-ups, and referrals to specialists when needed.

Maternity & Gynaecology

Antenatal check-ups, delivery support, postnatal care and gynaecological consultations from a dedicated team of midwives and doctors.

Paediatrics

Growth monitoring, immunisation and treatment for infants, toddlers and older children.

Laboratory Services

On-site blood work, urinalysis and diagnostic testing with same-day results in most cases.

Pharmacy

An in-house dispensary stocked with common prescriptions so you don't need a second stop.

Minor Surgery

Day-case surgical procedures performed under close post-operative observation.

24/7 Emergency Line

A dedicated emergency number staffed around the clock for urgent situations.
